01. Problem Statement

Challenges in Conventional Roadside Assistance

📞

Subjective Articulation

Drivers in stressful breakdown situations struggle to describe mechanical failure symptoms accurately to human call center dispatchers.

🧰

Tooling Mismatch & High Repeat Trips

Mechanics frequently arrive with incorrect equipment (e.g., standard jumper cables for thermal radiator breaches), requiring expensive secondary tows.

⏳

Opaque Dispatch & Safety Hazards

Dispatch relies solely on rough proximity without skill matching or workload awareness, leaving vulnerable stranded drivers in dark, unsafe corridors.

02. System Scope

Engineering Scope Control & Functional Objectives

🎯 5 Governed Fault Domains

  • Battery / Electrical: Terminal voltage thresholds (<11.8V 🟠, <11.0V 🔴)
  • Engine Overheating: Coolant thermal monitoring (>100°C 🟠, >110°C 🔴)
  • Tyre / Puncture: 4-wheel independent TPMS pressure checks (<25 PSI 🟠, <20 PSI 🔴)
  • Fuel System: Tank level depletion & starvation stall recognition (<5% 🟠, 0% 🔴)
  • Braking & Unknown Noise: Failsafe critical triage protocol (Always 🔴 Stop Now)

💻 Hardware Simulation Architecture

To remove dependency on physical OBD-II Bluetooth hardware during evaluation, the architecture incorporates an interactive parameter simulator injecting high-fidelity telematics scenarios.

Parameters: Voltage (V) · Coolant (°C) · 4-Corner PSI · Fuel (%) · Engine RPM · 8 Dashboard Warning Indicators

04. Fault Diagnosis

Explainable Rule Engine & Severity Triage

🟢 Green Triage

Safe to Drive Slowly

Minor voltage drops (11.8V-12.4V) or under-inflated tyres (25-28 PSI). Safe for slow travel to the nearest service garage.

🟠 Orange Triage

Stop Soon / Limited Driving

Coolant temperatures (100°C-110°C) or low fuel (<5%). Pull over safely to avert internal combustion damage.

🔴 Red Triage

Stop NOW / Immediate Shutdown

Coolant >110°C, flat tyre <15 PSI, battery <11V, or any brake warning. Hazard lights on, engine shut off immediately.

Pre-Arrival Mechanic Checklist: Auto-generates exact tools & parts manifest (multimeter, jumper, OAT coolant, radial plugs, fuel canisters) to guarantee first-visit resolution.
05. Algorithmic Dispatch

Smart Dispatch Mathematical Formulation

Utility Score = (0.40 × S_dist) + (0.25 × S_skill) + (0.20 × S_rating) + (0.15 × S_workload)
1. Haversine Proximity Factor (40%) Great-circle distance between breakdown GPS and mechanic position: max(0, 1 - d / 25km)
2. Skill Specialization Match (25%) Evaluates mechanic profile skills against diagnosed failure: 1.0 if match else 0.3
3. Rating Quality Score (20%) Normalizes verified customer review score: Rating / 5.0
4. Workload Availability (15%) Active concurrent job capacity: max(0, 1 - ActiveJobs / 5)
Auto-Cascade Dispatch: Dispatches top-scoring mechanic with a 60-second acceptance timeout before automatically cascading to the next optimal responder.

Subsystem Health Degradation Predictor

Electrical & Battery:25% Weight
Engine Thermal Circuit:25% Weight
Tyre Pressure Integrity:20% Weight

📈 60-Second Derivative Slope Analysis

Analyses telemetry slope from the Breakdown Black Box to forecast failure velocity:

Outputs concrete preventative actions before engine seizure or manifold warping occurs.
